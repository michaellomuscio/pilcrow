The ledger was a school exercise book, and it went back further than the surveys did.

Every entry was a date and a distance. *4 March 1949 — eleven feet, north-north-east.* *17 September 1953 — four feet, east.* The handwriting changed three times, which meant three people had kept it, which meant somebody had thought to hand it on.

"It doesn't move fast," Mrs. Aiken said, as though this were a reassurance. "You'd not notice, living here."

"Villages don't move at all."

"No," Mrs. Aiken agreed pleasantly, and poured the tea.
