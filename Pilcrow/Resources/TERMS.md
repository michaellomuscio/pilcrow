## 1. What Pilcrow is

Pilcrow is a book-writing application for macOS. It stores your work as plain
Markdown files in a folder you choose on your own computer.

It has no account system. It does not transmit your writing anywhere. It has
no analytics, no telemetry, and no network connection of any kind for your
manuscript content. Optional features that touch the rest of your machine —
adding an event to your calendar, reading a passage aloud — run locally and
only when you ask for them.

Pilcrow is offered free of charge. There is no purchase, no subscription, and
no paid tier.
