Her supervisor listened to all of it without interrupting, which was how she knew he had stopped listening somewhere in the first minute.

"Send me the coordinates," he said. "We'll flag it for the next revision."

"The next revision is in six years."

"Then it'll be six years."

She thought about forty-one houses and a repainted sign, and about a woman with a school exercise book who had been right for longer than the office had been wrong.
