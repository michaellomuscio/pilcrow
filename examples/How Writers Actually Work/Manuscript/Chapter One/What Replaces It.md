If the daily rule is out, something has to take its place, and the honest answer is that the replacement is smaller and more personal than a rule.

Sword's alternative is a diagnostic rather than a prescription: writing practice has behavioural, artisanal, social, and emotional dimensions, and a blocked writer is usually starved in one of them while over-investing in another [@sword2016].
